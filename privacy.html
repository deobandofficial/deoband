Privacy Policy

Last updated 25 October 2026

Who runs this

Deo Band runs this site for its members. The band's admins look after the data described here.

What is stored

  • Account: your name, instrument or role, username and password.
  • Band information: rehearsals, events, meetings, setlists and who plays what, attendance records, the pinned note, and the band name, tagline and logo.
  • Chat: your messages, replies, reactions, pins, and which messages you have read, so unread counts work.
  • Notifications: your notification choices, and one address per device that has notifications turned on.
  • In your browser: a login token that keeps you signed in for up to 90 days, and your theme choice. The site sets no cookies of its own and has no analytics or ads.

Passwords

Your password is checked on the server and kept as a salted hash for logging in. The site also keeps a copy that admins can reveal on the Band page. Choose a password you do not use on any other service.

Who can see what

  • Approved members see names, roles, chat, the schedule, setlists and everyone's attendance totals.
  • Admins also see usernames, waiting requests and member passwords.
  • The private Admins channel is visible to admins only.

Other services involved

  • Supabase hosts the database and stores everything listed above.
  • GitHub Pages serves the site files and sees the usual details of a web request, such as your IP address.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces. Your browser downloads them from Google, which sees your IP address and browser details.
  • Push services run by your browser or phone maker (Google, Apple or Mozilla) carry notifications to your device, so notification text passes through them.

How long it is kept

Login sessions expire after 90 days. Everything else stays until an admin deletes it or the site is closed. Removing a member deletes their profile, chat messages and notification addresses. Logging out removes the notification address for that device.

Your choices

  • Change your password and manage notifications, including muting channels, in Settings.
  • Ask an admin to correct your details or remove your account.
  • Log out on shared devices.

Security

The site uses HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure, so share only what you are comfortable with other members and admins seeing.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. Questions go to an admin through the Chat page. The rules for using the site are in the Terms of Use.
